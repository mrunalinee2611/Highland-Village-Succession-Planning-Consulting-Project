# Highland-Village-Succession-Planning-Consulting-Project

## Project Overview

This consulting project was developed for the **City of Highland Village, Texas**, to evaluate workforce readiness, succession risks, and leadership development opportunities within the **Fire Department** and **Parks & Recreation Department**.

The project focused on analyzing workforce data, employee readiness, job requirements, certifications, career pathways, and potential successors to identify leadership gaps and develop practical succession planning recommendations.

## Business Problem

Organizations can face significant operational risk when critical positions do not have qualified employees ready to step into those roles.

The City of Highland Village needed a structured approach to understand:

- Which positions presented the greatest succession risk
- Which employees could potentially progress into higher-level roles
- Which employees were **Ready Now** versus still **Developing**
- What certifications, training, and experience were required
- Where leadership pipeline gaps existed
- How the organization could better prepare employees for future roles

## Key Findings

The analysis identified several important succession planning challenges.

### 1. Leadership Readiness Gaps

Several employees showed potential for advancement but were not immediately ready to assume higher-level positions due to gaps in **certifications, training, experience, or leadership development**.

### 2. Limited Succession Depth

Some specialized and leadership positions had a limited number of qualified potential successors, creating increased workforce risk if an employee left unexpectedly.

### 3. Fire Department Pipeline Bottlenecks

The analysis identified bottlenecks within the Fire Department's leadership pipeline, particularly where advancement depended on specific certifications, experience, and training requirements.

### 4. Parks & Recreation Succession Gaps

Some Parks & Recreation career pathways had limited succession depth, highlighting opportunities to strengthen employee development and internal mobility.

## Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the team developed recommendations to strengthen the City's long-term talent pipeline.

### Employee Development

- Create **Individual Development Plans (IDPs)**
- Establish structured mentorship programs
- Provide additional leadership development opportunities
- Introduce acting-leadership assignments
- Expand job shadowing opportunities

### Cross-Training & Knowledge Transfer

- Increase cross-training between employees
- Develop knowledge-transfer plans for critical positions
- Encourage employees to gain experience across related functions
- Reduce dependency on single individuals for specialized knowledge

### Certification & Training

- Support employees in obtaining required certifications
- Provide access to relevant training programs
- Establish clearer development requirements for advancement
- Strengthen partnerships with regional training organizations

### Succession Planning

- Create clearer career and promotion pathways
- Maintain updated succession plans for critical positions
- Review succession readiness on a regular basis
- Track employees progressing from **Developing** to **Ready Now**
- Conduct annual succession-readiness assessments
